The Studio

Painted slowly, in a room that faces north light.

Senens began as a single easel and a stack of unprimed linen. Everything here is made by one pair of hands — no prints run off a press, no reproductions pretending to be originals.

When a piece leaves the studio, it travels in a hand-built crate, insured, to wherever it's going. A digital edition of the same work is offered for those who want the image without the freight.
